# Privacy policy — Remove Multi Titles (YouTube)

**Last updated:** 2026-10-04

## What this extension does

It remembers the title and thumbnail you see the first time for each video and keeps showing those if YouTube tries to show different ones later (for example during A/B tests). Everything needed for that happens on your device.

## Data we collect

**None.** There are no accounts, no analytics, and no requests to our servers. We do not receive your browsing data.

## Where information is stored

Pinned titles and thumbnail addresses (with the video ID and when you last saw the video) are stored only in your browser’s **local extension storage** (`storage` API). It stays on your computer and is not synced or uploaded by this extension. You can delete all of it at any time with **Clear pins** in the toolbar popup.

## Permissions

- **YouTube (`*://*.youtube.com/*`):** The extension only runs on YouTube sites so it can replace titles and thumbnails in the data YouTube loads for its own pages (watch page, Shorts, lists, YouTube Music). It does not run on other websites.
- **Storage:** Used only to save and read those pinned titles and thumbnails locally.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the project repository (see the extension listing or `homepage_url` in the manifest).

## Changes

If this policy changes in a future version, the updated text will be published at the same public URL you use for store listings, with an updated date at the top.
